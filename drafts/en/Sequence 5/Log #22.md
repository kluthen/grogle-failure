On the common channel, it's radio silence and nobody's moving either. I quickly understand why by focusing on the AR markers, or rather their absence: two survivors are dead — two pale green dots — and the others' dots don't show up anywhere. The Saturn 8's service access is open onto a canid's corpse, crushed by a rockfall. Beyond the entrance, there's nothing but darkness and the presence of unknown bracelets, but motionless.
