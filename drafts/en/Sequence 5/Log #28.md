# Log #28

Mike comes out of the dust the way you come out of a nightmare: too big, too slow, and already on you.

The Town Hall's facade no longer exists. In its place, a gutted concrete hole, twisted rebar dangling like torn tendons, and him in the middle, twenty meters off, shaking himself and raining down chunks of rubble the size of my torso. The pale morning lights him without mercy. I see everything. The bones piercing his fur and forming that metal-gray shell on his shoulders, his forearms, his ribcage. The gash behind the ear where the skin gave out under the growth. The two eyes that don't point the same way and that, nonetheless, give me the impression of seeing everything.

He lifts his head toward the heights he just fell from, then toward us. And he roars a name that isn't mine.

"FAAARAAAA."

Behind me, things jump. The creatures let themselves slide down from the top of the gray rampart ringing the square — the rampart that rose tonight while we slept, the one that was supposed to protect us and just locked us outside with the city's biggest predator. They drop on all fours, on two, every which way, and straighten up. Not an organized horde. Just everything Fortress mode trapped inside the perimeter as it raised its walls, and that has nowhere to go but onto us.

We're caught in a pincer. Mike in front. The rest behind.
