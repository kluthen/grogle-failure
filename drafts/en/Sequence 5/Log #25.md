# Log #25

The hum of the motors has settled into my bones. I've been wedged in the doorframe for a good while now, arms crossed, watching Fara and Santu hammer away at their AR interfaces. Their fingers churn the air in silence — lines of code visible to them alone. Santu shakes his head now and then before trying a new approach. Fara's like a sealed pressure cooker: nothing on the surface, the pressure rises all the same.

Crist's platform took off on its own thirty seconds after he shouted "We've got our means of transport." Santu can repair drones, not rewrite their route instructions. Fara's been looking for another solution since.

The back of the stockroom doesn't look much like it did when I jumped off the freight lift. Chahid moved the bags. Armande dozes against a shelf, her treated arm laid flat on her knees. Yannick crouches in front of his virtual otter, which sniffs his shoelaces with an expert's concentration. Julian's somewhere behind me — I hear his family-man voice comforting someone. As for Lou, she's carved herself a corner of the stockroom with Chahid and sorts the gear with that serious air she puts on when she wants to show she's useful. Seeing her move freely lowers a tension I hadn't noticed in my shoulders. It's not much. But it's enough.
