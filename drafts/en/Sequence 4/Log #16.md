# Log #16

Before setting our first foot outside, we all activate tactical mode. I see appear on the left side of my field of vision — like in any good action game — six little icons representing each member of the group. An estimate of health and fatigue is tied to them in the form of a bar. No mana, no stamina, no shield. I'm no big gamer, but I admit it would've gotten me pretty hyped to find this kind of info.  
I already picture the scene: a pack of canids charges at us. Julian throws a fireball to stop them. Twenty-five mana points consumed. Ten thousand damage points to their faces. The number displays in big. Mike and his clique are ready to break our teeth! Obviously, it's my turn to chuck a good fireball! I wiggle my little finger. Twenty-five mana points less and thirty-five thousand damage points. Mike dies open-mouthed, barbecue style, but a horde of amalgams surrounds us. Bam! Another fireball, or even two outright. A hundred thousand damage points. Smells like burning. The city catches fire. Too good, no? In fact, this kind of interface makes me want to have superpowers and solve all our problems with really nasty spells.  
As all six of us cram under the Saturn 8's porch, I crack up all alone at my own nonsense, surely because of the pressure starting to mount. I'm also a bit cramped. I feel Julian's breath on the back of my neck, and the two other guys in front of me keep me from seeing what's happening in the street. Fara orders us not to move. She observes the surroundings on her virtual screens.  
Raoul grumbles.

"You like all these corpses, is that it?" his buddy adds.

Just yesterday, these critters in suits and ties were heading to work in the offices on the corner and surely buying croissants at some luxury bakery, a bit like the one across the street.
